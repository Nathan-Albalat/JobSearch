## Instructions
Detect the language of the job description and write the cover letter in that same language (English if the offer is in English, French if in French).

Write a professional yet genuine cover letter (~350 words) with this structure:
1. **Hook** — An engaging opening that references something specific about the company or role (not generic)
2. **Why them** — Why this company/role is specifically interesting to the candidate
3. **Why me** — Highlight 2-3 concrete skills/experiences from the CV that match the offer's requirements
4. **Closing** — Availability (from March 15th 2027, minimum 22 weeks), enthusiasm, call to action

Tone: professional but not stiff — natural, direct, confident without being arrogant.

Output only the cover letter text in Markdown format (no preamble, no explanations, no "Here is your cover letter:").
Start directly with the opening paragraph. Do not use "—", prioritize the use of commas.
